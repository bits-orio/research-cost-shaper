Research Cost Shaper

Curve designer for the Factorio mod. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

You're looking at sample data. To see your own modpack, open script-output/research-cost-shaper/rcs-export.txt from your Factorio folder (the mod writes it every time Factorio starts), drop it anywhere on this page, or paste the text from /rcs-export in game.

Drag a point to move it. Double-click empty space to add a point, double-click a point to remove it. Coloured lines mark where each science pack is unlocked.
