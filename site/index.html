This is not your modpack

Everything below is Factorio's own techs, loaded as an example. To design a curve for your game, import the export the mod writes for your modpack:

  1. Start Factorio with Research Cost Shaper enabled. It writes rcs-export.txt every time Factorio starts.
  2. Find it in script-output/research-cost-shaper/ inside your Factorio folder (%APPDATA%\Factorio on Windows, ~/.factorio on Linux, ~/Library/Application Support/factorio on macOS).
  3. Import it here or drop it on the page. Or type /rcs-export in game and paste the text.
